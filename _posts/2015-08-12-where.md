# Where is it?

**We meet Queen Adelaide Village Hall on Ely Road just outside Ely**. The hall has ample space to play five to six games at once and is located at the edge of town.

## Is there somewhere I can park?

**Yes.** Parking is available on the field towards the back of the village hall; access road is next to the hall. Please use this and no longer park on the garage forecourt.
